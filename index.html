Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything a student, researcher, or founder needs to know about the AIC-GBU incubation process and platform.

Who can apply for incubation at AIC-GBU?
Any GBU student, alumnus, or faculty member with a technology-driven startup idea or early-stage venture may apply. External candidates from Noida-NCR region also qualify under the associate incubation program.
What facilities are available free of charge to incubatees?
Resident incubatees receive free access to co-working desks, IoT lab benches, high-performance GPU clusters, 3D printing (up to 2 kg/month), and broadband internet. Specialized equipment requires booking but is subsidized.
How does the seed grant process work?
Post formal incubation acceptance, startups are eligible for UP StartInUP prototype fund (up to ₹5 Lakh), NIDHI-PRAYAS grants (up to ₹10 Lakh), and SISFS (up to ₹20 Lakh). Disbursements are milestone-linked and tracked in the public ledger.
Can I participate in hackathons without being an incubatee?
Yes. All GBU students can register teams for hackathons. Winning teams receive direct fast-track incubation review along with prize money and DPIIT startup recognition assistance.
How long is the incubation program?
Standard cohort runs 18 months with option to extend by 6 months. Pre-incubation (Ideation track) is a 6-month structured program available to first-time founders.
